ESP32 Plant Vitals Monitor Diagrams

graph TD
    A[Soil Moisture Sensor] -->|Analog Data| ESP32
    B[DHT22 Sensor] -->|Digital Data| ESP32
    C[MiCS-5524 Gas Sensor] -->|Analog Data| ESP32
    D[Photoresistor] -->|Analog Data| ESP32
    ESP32 -->|Bluetooth Serial| Smartphone
    ESP32 -->|Control Signal| ArduinoUno
    ArduinoUno -->|Relay Control| WaterPump
    PowerBank --> ESP32
    PowerBank --> ArduinoUno
    Batteries --> WaterPump
    
flowchart TD
    Start([Start]) --> Init[Initialize Sensors and Bluetooth]
    Init --> CalibrateGas[Calibrate Gas Sensor (3 min)]
    CalibrateGas --> LoopStart{Loop every 2 seconds}
    
    LoopStart --> ReadSensors[Read all sensors]
    ReadSensors --> TransmitData[Send data via Bluetooth]
    TransmitData --> CheckMoisture{Soil Moisture < 50%?}
    
    CheckMoisture -- Yes --> PumpOn[Turn ON Water Pump]
    CheckMoisture -- No --> PumpOff[Turn OFF Water Pump]
    
    PumpOn --> Delay[Wait 2 seconds]
    PumpOff --> Delay
    
    Delay --> LoopStart
